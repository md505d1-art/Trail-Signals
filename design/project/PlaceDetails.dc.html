Larkhill Viewpoint

A grassy hilltop with benches and a long view west over the valley. Popular for sunsets and unhurried picnics.

Difficulty Moderate Steady 90 m climb; uneven steps near the top
Visit time 1.5–2 hours Includes the walk up from the stop and back
Path 2.4 km loop Gravel, then grass. Step-free to the lower meadow
Daylight Sunset 16:38 Start down by 16:50 to reach the stop in light
Route last checked 12 Oct by a Trail Signals volunteer. Paths, gates and signs can change — read recent visitor updates before you go.
